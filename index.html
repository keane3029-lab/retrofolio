how it goes

  1. 1

    pick a starting point

    four presets, or a blank desktop.

  2. 2

    fill it in

    projects, text files, and secret files that only unlock from the terminal.

  3. 3

    publish

    choose a handle and get a link. the copy-link option works without an account.

what visitors get

  • a boot sequence they can skip
  • windows they can drag and tap, even on a phone
  • a terminal with your own commands
  • a start menu with your links
